## Story Intent

As a Purchasing Manager, I want to create, revise, and approve Purchase Orders so that procurement is authorized before receiving and accounting matching.

## In-scope

- PO creation form (header + lines).
- PO list/detail views with status and open balance.
- Approval and revision actions with server-authoritative validation.
- Cancellation flow for eligible states.
- Read-only receipt progress indicators on PO detail.

## Out-of-scope

- Vendor invoice posting and payment.
- Accounting journal maintenance screens.
- Product master and vendor onboarding management.

## API Wiring (Expected)

- `createPurchaseOrder`
- `listPurchaseOrders`
- `getPurchaseOrder`
- `approvePurchaseOrder`
- `revisePurchaseOrder`
- `cancelPurchaseOrder`
- `receivePurchaseOrder`
