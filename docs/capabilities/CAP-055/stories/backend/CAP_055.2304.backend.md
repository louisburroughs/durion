## Story Intent
**As a** controller who approves bank reconciliations prepared by an accountant, **I want** a submit → approve workflow with separated duties, an immutable stored audit trail, automatic invalidation when the ledger inside an approved window changes, and published facts, **so that** an approved reconciliation is a trustworthy, non-editable attestation that is corrected only by an explicit superseding reconciliation (§3.9, §4.9, §5.6).

## Functional behaviour
EXISTING (F2, kept): `POST /{id}/finalize` refuses `abs(difference) > 0.01` with 422 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` and a `FINALIZED` mutation with 409 `RECONCILIATION_ALREADY_FINALIZED` (§1.1); `GET /{id}/audit` derives IMPORT / MATCH / ADJUSTMENT / FINALIZE from surviving rows (§1.1, G3); `reverseJournalEntry` never consults matches (§1.2, G5); no reconciliation writer of `AccountingAuditLog` exists (§1.2).

PROPOSED:
1. **Submit** (preparer, `adjust`): `IN_PROGRESS` only; E4 pre-check with the same refusals as approve; sets `SUBMITTED`, `submittedAt/By`; audit `RECONCILIATION_SUBMIT`; fact `.submitted` (§4.9).
2. **Approve** (`POST /{id}/finalize`, `approve`): must be `SUBMITTED` (409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_SUBMITTED`); approver ≠ `submittedBy` unless self-approval is allowed (403 `RECONCILIATION_SELF_APPROVAL`, pending D3); locks the reconciliation row `FOR UPDATE`, recomputes the live `glEndingBalance` and every E3 term inside the transaction, re-evaluates E4; on success `FINALIZED`, `finalizedAt/By`, `approvedGlEndingBalance` snapshot, matches sealed, audit `RECONCILIATION_APPROVE`, fact `.approved` (§4.9, I3).
3. **Return** (approver, reason): `SUBMITTED → IN_PROGRESS`; audit row; from any other state 409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_SUBMITTED` (§4.9, §4.10).
4. **Cancel** (approver, justification): `IN_PROGRESS` / `SUBMITTED → CANCELLED` (closes G2); its active matches → `UNMATCHED` with `unmatchReason = RECONCILIATION_CANCELLED` (members `active = false`, bank rows back to `UNMATCHED`), `OPEN` outstanding items it registered → `RELEASED`; posted adjustments untouched (they are real JEs, reversed explicitly); fact `.cancelled` (§4.9).
5. **Supersede** (approver, justification ≥ 10) on `FINALIZED` or `INVALIDATED`: creates a new `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation for the same statement with `supersedesReconciliationId`; in the same transaction the predecessor's match members are released (`active = false`, headers keep their sealed state as history) and re-created in the successor as `PROPOSED` matches so the preparer re-confirms them; `OPEN` outstanding items are account-level (O2) and carry into the successor's E3 unchanged — nothing is re-registered; when the successor is approved, the predecessor becomes `SUPERSEDED` (`supersededByReconciliationId`, fact `.superseded`); both stay readable and the audit chain links them (§4.9 path 1 as amended). The partial unique on `(tenant_id, statement_id)` covers `IN_PROGRESS`/`SUBMITTED` only, so predecessor and successor coexist (§6.4).
6. **Ledger-change hook** (§5.5) — after `markReversed` in `reverseJournalEntry` and after the status flip in `postJournalEntry`, same transaction, as a Spring in-process event or a direct `BankReconciliationLedgerChangeService` call, never a Kafka fact. Reversal: every active match containing a line of the original → `BROKEN` (`brokenByJournalEntryId`); its bank members → `UNMATCHED`; every `OPEN` outstanding item on a line of the original → `VOIDED` (`voidedByJournalEntryId`); an owning `FINALIZED` reconciliation → `INVALIDATED` (`invalidationReason = LEDGER_LINE_REVERSED`, `invalidatedByJournalEntryId`, audit `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATE`, fact `.invalidated`). Posting: a cash line dated inside a `FINALIZED` window → that reconciliation `INVALIDATED` (`LEDGER_LINE_POSTED`). The reversal or posting itself proceeds (pending D11); its new cash line is a fresh unexplained ledger line.
7. **Stored trail**: every mutation of §4 on a reconciliation, match, outstanding item, adjustment or bank transaction writes exactly one `AccountingAuditLog` row (`entityType` ∈ `BANK_RECONCILIATION`, `RECONCILIATION_MATCH`, `OUTSTANDING_ITEM`, `BANK_TRANSACTION`; `entityId`, operation, `userId`, `timestamp`, `traceId`, `justification`, `oldValue` / `newValue` as the status pair or the match / item id). `GET /{id}/audit` pages those rows for the reconciliation and its children (§4.9).
8. **Reopening a period** changes no reconciliation and invalidates nothing (§5.6) — no code, pinned by a test.

## Business rules and invariants
- **E4** at submit and approve: `|difference| ≤ 0.01` and `countUnexplainedBank = 0` and `countUnexplainedLedger = 0` and `openingDifference = 0`; the three counts are pending D2 — under a balance-only ruling they are reported, not enforced (§3.7).
- **I3** approval re-reads the live balance under the reconciliation row lock in its own transaction (§5.8).
- **I4** any later posting into a closed window is refused or audited as an override and invalidates the covering approval (§5.8) — the hook is the second half of that rule.
- **M7** matches are never deleted; a `FINALIZED` reconciliation's matches are sealed; `BROKEN` is the only transition the system makes on them (§3.4, §3.8).
- **O1 / O2** a `VOIDED` item leaves every later E3; `RELEASED` only while the registering reconciliation is not `FINALIZED` (§3.6).
- `FINALIZED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `CANCELLED` are terminal; `INVALIDATED` is terminal for editing and exists to be superseded (§3.8).
- Justification minimum 10 characters on return, cancel, supersede (pending D15).
- ADR-0018 actors from `SecurityContextHelper`; ADR-0044 §4 facts through `config/OutboxEventWriter.publish("accounting.events.v1", envelope)` in the state-changing transaction; ADR-0017 §2: 403 for who the caller is, 409 for lifecycle.

## Data requirements

- `bank_reconciliation_match.broken_by_journal_entry_id` and `state = BROKEN`; `bank_reconciliation_outstanding_item.voided_by_journal_entry_id`, `status ∈ VOIDED, RELEASED`; the `active` flags of the member tables are cleared on `BROKEN` / release.
- `accounting_audit_log`: new `entity_type` values above; INDEX `(tenant_id, entity_type, entity_id, timestamp)` if absent — the only DDL this story may add (`V3__…`, or folded per the flatten policy of §6.4).
- Tenancy per ADR-0062 §9: every row carries `tenant_id` under RLS; the hook and the audit reader run inside the request's tenant binding; `TenantIsolationIT` proves a second tenant reads no audit row.

## State model
`IN_PROGRESS → SUBMITTED` (submit) · `SUBMITTED → IN_PROGRESS` (return) · `SUBMITTED → FINALIZED` (approve) · `IN_PROGRESS | SUBMITTED → CANCELLED` (cancel) · `FINALIZED → INVALIDATED` (hook) · `FINALIZED | INVALIDATED → SUPERSEDED` (successor approved). EXISTING `IN_PROGRESS → FINALIZED` survives only as submit + approve by one actor when D3 allows it (§3.8). Match: `ACCEPTED → BROKEN` (hook); `PROPOSED | ACCEPTED → UNMATCHED` (`RECONCILIATION_CANCELLED`) on cancel. Outstanding item: `OPEN → VOIDED` (hook); `OPEN → RELEASED` (cancel).

## Alternate and error flows
- Approve after a JE dated inside the window posted between submit and approve: the live gate recomputes; if the difference moved, 422 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` and the review shows the new unexplained ledger line — nothing is approved on a stale figure (§4.9, fixes G4).
- Mutation on `INVALIDATED` / `SUPERSEDED` / `CANCELLED` → 409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_EDITABLE`; on `FINALIZED` → 409 `RECONCILIATION_ALREADY_FINALIZED` (§4.9).
- Self-approval with `BANK_REC_ALLOW_SELF_APPROVAL` false → 403 `RECONCILIATION_SELF_APPROVAL` and an audit row with the refused outcome; with it true → allowed and the audit row records that the switch was used (D3 recommendation).
- Adjustment reversal through the S4 endpoint: the hook unmatches the `ADJUSTMENT` match and invalidates a `FINALIZED` owner (§4.9 path 2); a repeat → 409 `ADJUSTMENT_ALREADY_REVERSED`.
- Override posting into a closed month inside a `FINALIZED` window (EXISTING `PERIOD_OVERRIDE_POST` audit) → the same `LEDGER_LINE_POSTED` invalidation (§5.5).
- Reopen of the period → nothing; the next posting into the window is what invalidates (§5.6).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Given** an `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation passing E4 **When** the preparer submits **Then** `SUBMITTED`, `submittedBy` = preparer, one `RECONCILIATION_SUBMIT` audit row and one `.submitted` outbox row in the same transaction (§4.9, §3.10).
2. **Given** a `SUBMITTED` reconciliation **When** a different user holding `approve` finalizes **Then** `FINALIZED`; `approvedGlEndingBalance` equals the live balance as-of `statementEndDate` at that instant; audit `RECONCILIATION_APPROVE`; fact `.approved` carries `approvedGlEndingBalance`, `adjustedBankBalance`, `accountingPeriodCode` (§4.9, E3).
3. **Given** the submitter approves with self-approval disabled **Then** 403 `RECONCILIATION_SELF_APPROVAL` and an audit row records the refusal (§4.9, pending D3).
4. **Given** an `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation **When** `finalize` or `return` is called **Then** 409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_SUBMITTED` (§4.10).
5. **Given** approval and a posting dated inside the window race **Then** in either order the end state is `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` or `INVALIDATED`, never a `FINALIZED` reconciliation whose `approvedGlEndingBalance` differs from the live balance (§8.4, I3).
6. **Given** a `FINALIZED` reconciliation with an `ACCEPTED` match on ledger line L and an `OPEN` deposit in transit on line D of the same JE **When** that JE is reversed **Then** the reversal succeeds; the match is `BROKEN` with `brokenByJournalEntryId`; its bank member is `UNMATCHED`; the item is `VOIDED`; the reconciliation is `INVALIDATED` (`LEDGER_LINE_REVERSED`) with audit `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATE` and fact `.invalidated` carrying the `journalEntryId` (§5.5, pending D11).
7. **Given** a `FINALIZED` window **When** a JE with a cash line dated inside it is posted (open, reopened or override) **Then** `INVALIDATED` with `LEDGER_LINE_POSTED` and `invalidatedByJournalEntryId` (§5.5).
8. **Given** a closed period is reopened **Then** no reconciliation changes; a subsequent posting into the window invalidates the covering approval (§5.6, §8.4).
9. **Given** an `INVALIDATED` reconciliation **When** superseded **Then** a new `IN_PROGRESS` record carries `supersedesReconciliationId`, the predecessor's members are `active = false`, its matches are re-created as `PROPOSED` in the successor, `OPEN` items are untouched and appear in the successor's E3; **When** the successor is approved **Then** the predecessor is `SUPERSEDED` with `supersededByReconciliationId` and fact `.superseded`; both readable; the `gl_line_id` and `statement_id` partial uniques hold throughout (§4.9, §6.4, §8.4).
10. **Given** a `SUBMITTED` reconciliation **When** cancelled with a justification **Then** `CANCELLED`, its matches `UNMATCHED` with `unmatchReason = RECONCILIATION_CANCELLED`, bank members `UNMATCHED`, its `OPEN` items `RELEASED`, posted adjustments unchanged, fact `.cancelled` (§4.9).
11. **Given** matches were unmatched and proposals rejected **When** `GET /{id}/audit` is read **Then** those rows appear with actor, `traceId`, operation and reason; removing any single audit write fails the trail test (§8.4, G3).
12. **Given** any S5 endpoint **When** called without its permission **Then** 403 with no existence leak; a second tenant reading the audit sees nothing (§8.4).

## Audit and observability
- `AccountingAuditLog` operations (§4 preamble naming rule — the `@EmitEvent` id without `ACCOUNTING_`): `RECONCILIATION_SUBMIT`, `RECONCILIATION_APPROVE` (on the `_FINALIZE` endpoint, named by §4.9), `RECONCILIATION_RETURN`, `RECONCILIATION_CANCEL`, `RECONCILIATION_SUPERSEDE`, `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATE` (system-initiated, `userId` = the actor of the triggering posting or reversal); `RECONCILIATION_UNMATCH` is S4's.
- `@EmitEvent` (§3.10, `config/EventTypes.java`): NEW `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_SUBMIT` (write), `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_RETURN` (write), `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_SUPERSEDE` (approval), `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_CANCEL` (approval); EXISTING kept: `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_FINALIZE` (now the approve step), `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_AUDIT`.
- Outbox facts on `accounting.events.v1`, `schemaVersion` 1, `aggregateId = reconciliationId`: `.submitted` (`glAccountId`, `statementEndDate`, `difference`, counts); `.approved` (`glAccountId`, `statementStartDate`, `statementEndDate`, `accountingPeriodCode`, `approvedGlEndingBalance`, `adjustedBankBalance`, `approvedBy`); `.invalidated` (`reason` ∈ `LEDGER_LINE_REVERSED`, `LEDGER_LINE_POSTED`, `SOURCE_REMOVED`; `journalEntryId` nullable); `.superseded` (`supersededByReconciliationId`); `.cancelled` (`reason`).
