## Issue Body

Part of the manual bank reconciliation specification — durion `domains/accounting/SPEC-manual-bank-reconciliation.md` (§7.1 story S5; PR louisburroughs/durion — https://github.com/louisburroughs/durion/pull/519). Capability CAP:055 (louisburroughs/durion#55). Domain: accounting.

## Story Intent
**As a** controller who approves bank reconciliations prepared by an accountant, **I want** a submit → approve workflow with separated duties and a matching-complete approval gate, an immutable stored audit trail, automatic invalidation when the ledger inside an approved window changes, and published facts, **so that** an approved reconciliation is a trustworthy, non-editable attestation that every bank transaction and ledger line from the account baseline to the window end is explained, corrected only by an explicit superseding reconciliation (§3.9, §4.9, §5.6).

## Functional behaviour
EXISTING (F2, kept): `POST /{id}/finalize` refuses `abs(difference) > 0.01` with 422 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` and a `FINALIZED` mutation with 409 `RECONCILIATION_ALREADY_FINALIZED` (§1.1); `GET /{id}/audit` derives IMPORT / MATCH / ADJUSTMENT / FINALIZE from surviving rows (§1.1, G3); `reverseJournalEntry` never consults matches (§1.2, G5); no reconciliation writer of `AccountingAuditLog` exists (§1.2).

PROPOSED:
1. **Submit** (preparer, `adjust`): `IN_PROGRESS` only; E4 pre-check with the same refusals as approve; sets `SUBMITTED`, `submittedAt/By`; audit `RECONCILIATION_SUBMIT`; fact `.submitted` (§4.9).
2. **Approve** (`POST /{id}/finalize`, `approve`): must be `SUBMITTED` (409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_SUBMITTED`); approver ≠ `submittedBy` unless `BANK_REC_ALLOW_SELF_APPROVAL` is true (else 403 `RECONCILIATION_SELF_APPROVAL`, D3); an approval made under the switch is audited as a self-approval, the row naming the same user as submitter and approver; locks the reconciliation row `FOR UPDATE`, recomputes the live `glEndingBalance`, every E3 term, `baselineDate` and both unexplained counts inside the transaction and re-evaluates E4; on success `FINALIZED`, `finalizedAt/By`, `approvedGlEndingBalance` and `baselineDate` snapshots, matches sealed, audit `RECONCILIATION_APPROVE`, fact `.approved` (§4.9, I3).
3. **E4 evaluation** (§3.7, D2), identical at submit and approve: (a) `|difference| > 0.01` → 422 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` with `fieldErrors[difference]`; else (b) `countUnexplainedBank > 0` or `countUnexplainedLedger > 0` → 422 `RECONCILIATION_HAS_UNEXPLAINED_ITEMS` with both counts and the first 50 ids per side in `fieldErrors`. The counts are exact and run from `baselineDate` to `statementEndDate` (unreviewed `POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE` rows, late arrivals — phase 2 only, D10 — and aged `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING` items not reaffirmed in this reconciliation count — S4 computes them). A non-zero `openingDifference` never refuses. The review's `canSubmit` / `canApprove` gain `SELF_APPROVAL` for `canApprove` (§4.8).
4. **Return** (approver, reason): `SUBMITTED → IN_PROGRESS`; audit row; from any other state 409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_SUBMITTED` (§4.9, §4.10).
5. **Cancel** (approver, justification): `IN_PROGRESS` / `SUBMITTED → CANCELLED` (closes G2); its active matches → `UNMATCHED` with `unmatchReason = RECONCILIATION_CANCELLED` (members `active = false`, bank rows back to `UNMATCHED`), `OPEN` outstanding items it registered → `RELEASED`; posted adjustments untouched (they are real JEs, reversed explicitly); fact `.cancelled` (§4.9).
6. **Supersede** (approver, justification ≥ 10) on `FINALIZED` or `INVALIDATED`: creates a new `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation for the same statement with `supersedesReconciliationId`; in the same transaction the predecessor's match members are released (`active = false`, headers keep their sealed state as history) and re-created in the successor as `PROPOSED` matches so the preparer re-confirms them; `OPEN` outstanding items are account-level (O2) and carry into the successor's E3 unchanged — nothing is re-registered; the successor uses the baseline that governed its window, not the current one, and inherits a posted gap bridge through the ledger (§3.1); when the successor is approved, the predecessor becomes `SUPERSEDED` (`supersededByReconciliationId`, fact `.superseded`); both stay readable and the audit chain links them (§4.9 path 1). The partial unique on `(tenant_id, statement_id)` covers `IN_PROGRESS`/`SUBMITTED` only, so predecessor and successor coexist (§6.4).
7. **Ledger-change hook** (§5.5) — after `markReversed` in `reverseJournalEntry` and after the status flip in `postJournalEntry`, same transaction, as a Spring in-process event or a direct `BankReconciliationLedgerChangeService` call, never a Kafka fact. Reversal: every active match containing a line of the original → `BROKEN` (`brokenByJournalEntryId`) — incl. the replacement match of a reversed residual settlement, whose members return to `UNMATCHED` (§4.9 path 2); its bank members → `UNMATCHED`; every `OPEN` outstanding item on a line of the original → `VOIDED` (`voidedByJournalEntryId`, `closedOn` = the reversal's `transactionDate`, §3.6); an owning `FINALIZED` reconciliation → `INVALIDATED` (`invalidationReason = LEDGER_LINE_REVERSED`, `invalidatedByJournalEntryId`, audit `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATE`, fact `.invalidated`). Posting: a cash line dated inside a `FINALIZED` window → that reconciliation `INVALIDATED` (`LEDGER_LINE_POSTED`) — incl. a `TRANSFER`'s counter cash line on the counter account (§3.5). The reversal or posting itself proceeds (D11). The original's line and the reversal's line form a reversal pair that never counts as unexplained (§3.7); what the original explained at the bank — its bank members, now `UNMATCHED` — counts again and shows in `difference` until re-explained. The hook reacts to ledger changes only, never to bank data (D10).
8. **Stored trail**: every mutation of §4 on a reconciliation, match, outstanding item, adjustment or bank transaction writes exactly one `AccountingAuditLog` row (`entityType` ∈ `BANK_RECONCILIATION`, `RECONCILIATION_MATCH`, `OUTSTANDING_ITEM`, `BANK_TRANSACTION`; `entityId`, operation, `userId`, `timestamp`, `traceId`, `justification`, `oldValue` / `newValue` as the status pair or the match / item id). `GET /{id}/audit` pages those rows for the reconciliation and its children (§4.9).
9. **Reopening a period** changes no reconciliation and invalidates nothing (§5.6) — no code, pinned by a test.
10. **Statement supersession** (§4.9 path 3; `adjust` suffices — it approves nothing): the import create or manual statement names a COMMITTED statement of the same account in `supersedesStatementId` and carries `supersessionJustification` (≥ 10, D15; absent or blank → 400 `VALIDATION_ERROR`, 1–9 characters → 400 `JUSTIFICATION_REQUIRED`); a named statement unknown in the tenant, of another GL account or not `COMMITTED` → 422 `STATEMENT_SUPERSESSION_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; refused with 409 `RECONCILIATION_WINDOW_ALREADY_RECONCILED` while that statement has an `IN_PROGRESS` or `SUBMITTED` reconciliation; both checked at commit, and for an import also at upload. In the commit transaction: the old statement → `SUPERSEDED` with `supersededByStatementId`, audited as one row (`entityType = BANK_STATEMENT`, `entityId` = the old statement, operation `BANK_STATEMENT_SUPERSEDE`, `oldValue` / `newValue` = `COMMITTED` / `SUPERSEDED`, `justification` = `supersessionJustification`, the request's `traceId`); its bank transactions → `EXCLUDED` with `exclusionReason = STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED` (R2; no intake collision with the corrected rows, §4.5; `restore` refused for them, §3.8); a `FINALIZED` reconciliation of it → `INVALIDATED` (`invalidationReason = STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED`, audit `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATE`, fact `.invalidated`) with its match members released as supersede releases them; bank-side `OPEN` items on the old rows → `RELEASED` with reason `STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED` (ledger-side items stay, O2); the corrected statement passes E1, U1–U2 and E2 with the old one left out; the intake recomputes the baseline (audited only if it changes). A new reconciliation is created against the corrected statement; when it is `FINALIZED` it sets `supersedesReconciliationId` to the invalidated one, which becomes `SUPERSEDED`.

## Business rules and invariants
- **E4** (D2) at submit and approve: `|difference| ≤ 0.01` **and** `countUnexplainedBank = 0` **and** `countUnexplainedLedger = 0`, checked in that order; `openingDifference` is reported, never gated (§3.7). This supersedes only the balance-only sentence of ruling (1) of 2026-07-20; the identity, the ±0.01 tolerance and the exclusion of `Σ matched` stand (§1.5 C1).
- **I3** approval re-reads the live balance and the counts under the reconciliation row lock in its own transaction (§5.8).
- **I4** any later posting into a closed window is refused or audited as an override and invalidates the covering approval (§5.8) — the hook is the second half of that rule.
- **M7** matches are never deleted; a `FINALIZED` reconciliation's matches are sealed; `BROKEN` is the only transition the system makes on them (§3.4, §3.8).
- **O1 / O2** a `VOIDED` item leaves every later E3 from its `closedOn`; `RELEASED` only while the registering reconciliation is not `FINALIZED` (§3.6).
- `FINALIZED`, `SUPERSEDED`, `CANCELLED` are terminal; `INVALIDATED` is terminal for editing and exists to be superseded (§3.8).
- Justification minimum 10 characters on return, cancel, supersede and statement supersession (D15).
- ADR-0018 actors from `SecurityContextHelper`; ADR-0044 §4 facts through `config/OutboxEventWriter.publish("accounting.events.v1", envelope)` in the state-changing transaction; ADR-0017 §2: 403 for who the caller is, 409 for lifecycle, 422 for the E4 refusals.

## State model
`IN_PROGRESS → SUBMITTED` (submit, E4) · `SUBMITTED → IN_PROGRESS` (return) · `SUBMITTED → FINALIZED` (approve, E4) · `IN_PROGRESS | SUBMITTED → CANCELLED` (cancel) · `FINALIZED → INVALIDATED` (hook, or its statement superseded) · `FINALIZED | INVALIDATED → SUPERSEDED` (successor approved). EXISTING `IN_PROGRESS → FINALIZED` survives only as submit + approve by one actor when `BANK_REC_ALLOW_SELF_APPROVAL` is true (§3.8, D3). Match: `ACCEPTED → BROKEN` (hook); `PROPOSED | ACCEPTED → UNMATCHED` (`RECONCILIATION_CANCELLED`) on cancel. Outstanding item: `OPEN → VOIDED` (hook, `closedOn`); `OPEN → RELEASED` (cancel; bank-side items on a superseded statement's rows). Bank statement: `COMMITTED → SUPERSEDED`; its bank transactions `→ EXCLUDED` (`STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED`, system).

## Alternate and error flows
- Approve after a JE dated inside the window posted between submit and approve: the live gate recomputes; if the difference moved, 422 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED`; if it still balances but the new line is unexplained, 422 `RECONCILIATION_HAS_UNEXPLAINED_ITEMS` naming it — nothing is approved on a stale figure (§4.9, fixes G4).
- A difference beyond ±0.01 **and** unexplained items → only `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` (E4 order, §3.7).
- A predecessor `INVALIDATED` so `openingDifference` is non-zero, everything in the window explained, `|difference| ≤ 0.01` → submit and approve succeed; the review shows the diagnostic (§3.7, §8.1).
- An unreviewed `POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE`, an aged `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING` item not reaffirmed here, or (phase 2) a late feed arrival (D10) → 422 `RECONCILIATION_HAS_UNEXPLAINED_ITEMS` until reviewed, explained or reaffirmed (§5.5, §3.6, §8.3, §8.4).
- A corrected re-import naming an unknown statement, another account's or a `SUPERSEDED` one → 422 `STATEMENT_SUPERSESSION_NOT_ELIGIBLE`, nothing changes. Over a statement with an `IN_PROGRESS` or `SUBMITTED` reconciliation → 409 `RECONCILIATION_WINDOW_ALREADY_RECONCILED` (finish or cancel it first); over one with a `FINALIZED` reconciliation → that reconciliation `INVALIDATED` (`STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED`) and period close blocked by `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATED` until a new approval supersedes it (§4.9 path 3, §5.3).
- Mutation on `INVALIDATED` / `SUPERSEDED` / `CANCELLED` → 409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_EDITABLE`; on `FINALIZED` → 409 `RECONCILIATION_ALREADY_FINALIZED` (§4.9).
- Self-approval with `BANK_REC_ALLOW_SELF_APPROVAL` false → 403 `RECONCILIATION_SELF_APPROVAL` and an audit row with the refused outcome; with it true → allowed and audited as a self-approval naming the same user as submitter and approver (§4.9, D3).
- Adjustment reversal through the S4 endpoint: the hook unmatches the `ADJUSTMENT` match, breaks a residual's replacement match, and invalidates a `FINALIZED` owner (§4.9 path 2); a repeat → 409 `ADJUSTMENT_ALREADY_REVERSED`.
- Override posting into a closed month inside a `FINALIZED` window (EXISTING `PERIOD_OVERRIDE_POST` audit) → the same `LEDGER_LINE_POSTED` invalidation (§5.5).
- Reopen of the period → nothing; the next posting into the window is what invalidates (§5.6).

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Given** an `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation passing E4 **When** the preparer submits **Then** `SUBMITTED`, `submittedBy` = preparer, one `RECONCILIATION_SUBMIT` audit row and one `.submitted` outbox row in the same transaction (§4.9, §3.10).
2. **Given** a `SUBMITTED` reconciliation **When** a different user holding `approve` finalizes **Then** `FINALIZED`; `approvedGlEndingBalance` equals the live balance as-of `statementEndDate` at that instant and `baselineDate` is snapshotted; audit `RECONCILIATION_APPROVE`; fact `.approved` carries `approvedGlEndingBalance`, `adjustedBankBalance`, `accountingPeriodCode` (§4.9, §3.7).
3. **Given** a window with `difference = −0.05` and 3 unexplained ledger lines **When** submitted **Then** 422 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` with `fieldErrors[difference]`; **Given** `difference = 0.00` and the same 3 lines **Then** 422 `RECONCILIATION_HAS_UNEXPLAINED_ITEMS` with `countUnexplainedLedger = 3`, `countUnexplainedBank = 0` and the 3 line ids in `fieldErrors` (§3.7, §4.10).
4. **Given** `openingDifference` non-zero because the previous window is `INVALIDATED`, and E4 otherwise met **When** submitted and approved **Then** both succeed [M] — make the opening difference a gate condition and the test fails (§8.1).
5. **Given** a deposit in transit left unregistered (`countUnexplainedLedger = 1`), or an aged `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING` item not reaffirmed in this reconciliation **When** approved **Then** 422 `RECONCILIATION_HAS_UNEXPLAINED_ITEMS`; after registration / `reaffirm` approval succeeds (§3.6, §8.3 [M]).
6. **Given** a statement with a `FINALIZED` reconciliation **When** a corrected statement is imported with `supersedesStatementId` **Then** the old statement is `SUPERSEDED`, its rows `EXCLUDED` (`STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED`), the reconciliation `INVALIDATED` (`STATEMENT_SUPERSEDED`, fact `.invalidated`) with released members, and the corrected rows raise no duplicate collision with the old ones; **Given** an `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation instead **Then** 409 `RECONCILIATION_WINDOW_ALREADY_RECONCILED`; superseding the account's first (acknowledged) statement recomputes the baseline and writes `BANK_ACCOUNT_BASELINE_SET` only if the value changes; the old statement has exactly one `BANK_STATEMENT_SUPERSEDE` row (`COMMITTED` → `SUPERSEDED`, the justification, the request's `traceId`) [M] — drop the write and the trail test fails; naming another account's statement, a `SUPERSEDED` one or an unknown id → 422 `STATEMENT_SUPERSESSION_NOT_ELIGIBLE` and nothing changes (§4.9 path 3, §8.4).
7. **Given** the submitter approves with self-approval disabled **Then** 403 `RECONCILIATION_SELF_APPROVAL` and an audit row records the refusal; **Given** it enabled **Then** `FINALIZED` and the audit row names the same user as submitter and approver (§4.9, D3).
8. **Given** an `IN_PROGRESS` reconciliation **When** `finalize` or `return` is called **Then** 409 `RECONCILIATION_NOT_SUBMITTED` (§4.10).
9. **Given** approval and a posting dated inside the window race **Then** in either order the end state is `RECONCILIATION_NOT_BALANCED` or `INVALIDATED`, never a `FINALIZED` reconciliation whose `approvedGlEndingBalance` differs from the live balance (§8.4, I3).
10. **Given** a `FINALIZED` reconciliation with an `ACCEPTED` match on ledger line L and an `OPEN` deposit in transit on line D of the same JE **When** that JE is reversed **Then** the reversal succeeds; the match is `BROKEN` with `brokenByJournalEntryId`; its bank member is `UNMATCHED` and counts as unexplained, while neither ledger line of the reversal pair does (§3.7); the item is `VOIDED` with `closedOn` = the reversal's `transactionDate`; the reconciliation is `INVALIDATED` (`LEDGER_LINE_REVERSED`) with audit `RECONCILIATION_INVALIDATE` and fact `.invalidated` carrying the `journalEntryId` (§5.5, §3.6, D11).
11. **Given** a residual settlement whose replacement match is `ACCEPTED` **When** its adjustment is reversed **Then** the replacement match is `BROKEN` and its members return to `UNMATCHED` (§4.9 path 2).
12. **Given** a `FINALIZED` window **When** a JE with a cash line dated inside it is posted (open, reopened or override) **Then** `INVALIDATED` with `LEDGER_LINE_POSTED` and `invalidatedByJournalEntryId`; **Given** a closed period is reopened **Then** no reconciliation changes until that posting (§5.5, §5.6, §8.4).
13. **Given** an `INVALIDATED` reconciliation **When** superseded **Then** a new `IN_PROGRESS` record carries `supersedesReconciliationId`, the predecessor's members are `active = false`, its matches are re-created as `PROPOSED`, `OPEN` items appear in the successor's E3, and the successor's `baselineDate` is the one that governed the window even if a later acknowledged statement moved the account baseline; **When** the successor is approved **Then** the predecessor is `SUPERSEDED` with `supersededByReconciliationId` and fact `.superseded`; the `gl_line_id` and `statement_id` partial uniques hold throughout (§4.9, §6.4, §8.2, §8.4).
14. **Given** a `SUBMITTED` reconciliation **When** cancelled with a justification **Then** `CANCELLED`, its matches `UNMATCHED` with `unmatchReason = RECONCILIATION_CANCELLED`, bank members `UNMATCHED`, its `OPEN` items `RELEASED`, posted adjustments unchanged, fact `.cancelled` (§4.9).
15. **Given** matches were unmatched and proposals rejected **When** `GET /{id}/audit` is read **Then** those rows appear with actor, `traceId`, operation and reason; removing any single audit write fails the trail test (§8.4, G3); any S5 endpoint without its permission → 403 with no existence leak; a second tenant reading the audit sees nothing (§8.4).
