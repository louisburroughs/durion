## Story Intent
**As a** preparer (holder of `accounting:reconciliation:adjust`) **I want** to start a reconciliation from a committed statement (an interim to a date being a manual-entry statement), see the live equation E3, the opening terms and every unexplained item from the account's reconciliation baseline on one review screen, match bank transactions to posted ledger lines (1:1, 1:N, N:1) from deterministic ranked candidates, register and re-judge non-posting outstanding items, and post typed or linked `OTHER` adjustments idempotently on the right date — including a match's rounding residual and an acknowledged gap's bridge — and reverse them explicitly **so that** a window with genuine timing differences finalizes with zero journal entries, every judgement and every entry to the 2360 clearing account rests on evidence and is recorded with its reasons, and no balance is ever computed from a stale snapshot.

EXISTING replaced — `POST /{id}/match` → `POST /{id}/matches`; `POST /{id}/unmatch` → `POST /{id}/matches/{matchId}/unmatch` (§6.1); ids `ACCOUNTING_RECONCILIATION_MATCH` / `_UNMATCH` kept on the new paths.
2. PROPOSED — create: from a COMMITTED statement (`statementId`, window and balances copied; `RECONCILIATION_WINDOW_ALREADY_RECONCILED` if an `IN_PROGRESS`/`SUBMITTED` reconciliation exists for it — S1 partial unique — or a `FINALIZED` one without a successor — service rule, §6.4; the correction path is supersede, S5); every phase-1 reconciliation rests on a COMMITTED statement — the interim to a date (§4.1 d, §5.7 b) is a manual-entry statement (S2) reconciled like any other, and the statementless interim body is phase 2 (§6.1, §7.3): in phase 1 it answers 422 `BANK_ACCOUNT_FEED_NOT_LINKED`, since no account has a feed link (§3.1, §4.10). `accountingPeriodCode` is attribution only (§5.7). Non-posting operations are allowed whatever the period state (§5.1).
3. PROPOSED — E3 (§3.7), F2 sign conventions (bank positive = cash in; ledger = debit − credit): `adjustedBankBalance = statementClosingBalance + Σ LEDGER-side items open at the end of statementEndDate − Σ BANK-side items open then`, where an item is open at the end of day d when `itemDate ≤ d`, `status ≠ RELEASED` and `closedOn` is null or later than d (§3.6); `adjustedBookBalance = glEndingBalance(live, entries POSTED or REVERSED, transactionDate ≤ statementEndDate 23:59:59.999999 via getAccountBalanceAsOf) + sumLateAdjustments`, `sumLateAdjustments` = the adjustment postings (an adjustment's JE cash line and, once reversed, its reversal JE's cash line) of this or an earlier reconciliation on the account (earlier = `statementEndDate` on/before this one's) dated after `statementEndDate`. Every balance read here — `glOpeningBalance`, `glEndingBalance` — counts each posted entry at its own `transactionDate`, so a reversed pair counts the original from its date and the inverse from the reversal's; this story widens EXISTING `getAccountBalanceAsOf` from `POSTED` to `POSTED` or `REVERSED` (§3.7, G15); `difference = adjustedBankBalance − adjustedBookBalance`. `Σ matched` stays out (C5).
4. PROPOSED — opening terms (diagnostic, D2): `glOpeningBalance` as-of `statementStartDate − 1`; `sumOpeningAdjustments` = Σ adjustment postings of reconciliations on the account with `statementEndDate < statementStartDate`, dated ≥ `statementStartDate`, plus the adjustment postings of this statement's gap bridges (a bridge and, if reversed, its reversal) dated ≥ `statementStartDate`; `openingDifference = statementOpeningBalance + Σ LEDGER-side items open at the end of statementStartDate − 1 − Σ BANK-side items open then − (glOpeningBalance + sumOpeningAdjustments)`. Within ±0.01 it reads as continuous; beyond it the review shows the flag `OPENING_DIFFERENCE` under Diagnostics with the likely cause (an `INVALIDATED` predecessor, or an acknowledged gap not yet bridged) — never a blocking reason.
5. PROPOSED — unexplained items (E4 inputs, D2): `baselineDate` = the `startDate` of the latest acknowledged statement whose `startDate` is on/before the window start (§3.1), stored on the row. Counts run from `baselineDate` to `statementEndDate`: bank — `UNMATCHED` or `POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE` rows (a row in a `PROPOSED` match is still `UNMATCHED`) in no `OPEN` bank-side item, incl. rows carried in from earlier windows and late arrivals (D10); ledger — lines of POSTED entries in no `ACCEPTED` match, not the cash line of a gap bridge, in no `OPEN` item, except that an aged `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING` item not reaffirmed in this reconciliation counts. **Reversal pairs never count** (§3.7): the REVERSED original is not POSTED and the POSTED entry reversing it is excluded too, matched or not — the pair nets to zero; what the original explained at the bank returns to `UNMATCHED` and counts on the bank side and in `difference` (a reversed gap bridge, a reversed adjustment, any reversed ledger JE). Counts are exact; ±0.01 applies only to amounts.
6. PROPOSED — candidates for a `bankTransactionId` (or the mirror for a `glLineId`): POSTED lines on the account, in no active match and no `OPEN` item, dated within `[transactionDate − W, transactionDate + W]` and never after `statementEndDate` (M3), scored `EXACT_AMOUNT` +60, `WITHIN_TOLERANCE` +40, `DATE_IN_WINDOW` +20 × (1 − d/W), `REFERENCE_MATCH` +20, `DESCRIPTION_SIMILAR` up to +10 (Jaccard over `normalizedDescription` tokens ≥ 0.5); `windowDays` widens explicitly and marks `DATE_OUT_OF_WINDOW`. Deterministic, no model. `auto-match`: for every unexplained bank transaction whose top candidate scores ≥ 90 and beats the second by ≥ 20, a `ONE_TO_ONE` match `origin = RULE`, `state = PROPOSED`; ties propose nothing (`ambiguousCount`). The system never creates an `ACCEPTED` match (M6, D12). **Near-duplicate candidates** of a row R under duplicate review (§4.5): bank transactions on the account other than R, not `EXCLUDED` or `REMOVED_BY_SOURCE`, with the same `signedAmount` (4 dp) and a `transactionDate` within ±`duplicate.date-window-days` of R's; ranked equal `reference` or `checkNumber` first, then equal `normalizedDescription`, then smaller date distance, then earlier `firstObservedAt`; the top five are served; they only propose originals, never change a status.
7. PROPOSED — human match `POST /{id}/matches` `{bankTransactionIds[], glLineIds[], justification?, requestId}`: `matchKind` from cardinality (N:M → `MATCH_CARDINALITY_NOT_ALLOWED`); M1 (else `MATCH_AMOUNT_MISMATCH`); `toleranceUsed = abs(bankTotal − ledgerTotal)`, and every match served with its signed `residual = bankTotal − ledgerTotal` (not stored, §3.4); M3/M4 eligibility (else `RECONCILIATION_LINE_INELIGIBLE` — incl. a ledger line dated after `statementEndDate`, since only the service creates the adjustment members M3 exempts; a `PENDING` row is never matchable, D19); M5 reasons → `MATCH_REQUIRES_REVIEW` with `fieldErrors[justification]` until a justification (≥ 10, D15) is supplied; created `ACCEPTED`, members `active = true`, bank rows `MATCHED`; any `OPEN` ledger-side item whose line is now matched → `CLEARED` (`clearedInReconciliationId`, `clearedByMatchId`, `clearedAt`, `closedOn` = the latest bank-member date) in the same transaction (§5.4). `accept` / `reject` act on `PROPOSED`; `unmatch` `{reason}` on `ACCEPTED` → `UNMATCHED`, members `active = false`, bank rows `UNMATCHED`, audit `RECONCILIATION_UNMATCH` (G3). Only while `IN_PROGRESS` (or `SUBMITTED`, S5); `FINALIZED` → `RECONCILIATION_ALREADY_FINALIZED`.

PROPOSED — outstanding items: `LEDGER` side (`DEPOSIT_IN_TRANSIT` > 0, `OUTSTANDING_CHECK` < 0, `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING`) on a POSTED line on the account dated ≤ window end, in no active match and no `OPEN` item — incl. pre-baseline lines still outstanding at an acknowledged statement (§3.1, §4.2 step 2); `BANK` side `BANK_ERROR_PENDING`; violations → `OUTSTANDING_ITEM_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; justification for `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING`, `BANK_ERROR_PENDING` and any item older than `aging-warning-days`; `release` `{reason}` → `RELEASED` only while the registering reconciliation is not `FINALIZED`. ## Business rules and invariants
- **E3** (behaviour 3) and the opening terms (behaviour 4); tolerance ±0.01 at 2 dp; amounts at 4 dp (§8.1). `openingDifference` is a diagnostic only (D2). Balances count entries at their own dates, never retroactively (I4, ADR-0047); reversal pairs never count as unexplained (§3.7).
- **M1** Σ bank − Σ ledger within ±0.01, a larger residual is an adjustment; `toleranceUsed = abs(bankTotal − ledgerTotal)`, the signed `residual` is served, never stored; **M2** exactly one side may have more than one member, not counting a residual member; **M3** every ledger member POSTED, on the account, dated ≤ `statementEndDate`, except this reconciliation's own adjustment cash line (`ADJUSTMENT` match or residual member), created only by the service when the adjustment posts, that D7 may date later and E3 carries in `sumLateAdjustments` — a human match naming a later-dated line gets 409 `RECONCILIATION_LINE_INELIGIBLE`; **M4** bank members `UNMATCHED` or reviewed-distinct, in no `OPEN` item, `settlementState = POSTED`, inside the window or carried in; **M5** justification for non-1:1, `toleranceUsed ≠ 0`, out-of-window dates or a former `POSSIBLE_DUPLICATE`; **M6** no system-`ACCEPTED` `RULE` match; **M7** matches are never deleted (§3.4).
- **O1** a line or bank transaction is in at most one `OPEN` item and never also in an active match; **O2** an item contributes to the closing terms of every reconciliation at whose end it is open and to the opening terms of every reconciliation at whose start it is open; **O3** registering posts nothing (§3.6, C2).
- Clearing-account governance (§4.7, D2): `OTHER` → 2360 is the only posting escape and is always linked, justified, threshold-checked and reported; never used for a timing difference, bank error, duplicate or books error.
- Adjustment date: the period governing an adjustment is the one containing its `transactionDate` (§5, C3, D7); every posting goes through `AccountingPeriodGate` (§5.9).
- Idempotency: UUIDv7 `requestId` on every command; same payload → `replayed: true`, different → `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` (§6.3, AD-010). Justification minimum 10 (D15); actor from `SecurityContextHelper` (ADR-0018).

## State model
- `ReconciliationMatch` (§3.4, §3.8): `PROPOSED → ACCEPTED | REJECTED`; `ACCEPTED → UNMATCHED` (human reason, or system `RESIDUAL_SETTLED` with an `ACCEPTED` replacement carrying `replacesMatchId`). `→ BROKEN` and sealing at `FINALIZED` are S5.
- `OutstandingItem` (§3.6): `OPEN → CLEARED` (later match, `closedOn` = latest bank-member date) | `CLEARED_IN_GAP` (`clear-in-gap`, `closedOn = startDate − 1`) | `RELEASED`; `reaffirm` changes no status. `→ VOIDED` is S5.
- `ReconciliationAdjustment`: `POSTED → REVERSED` through `reverseJournalEntry`; the `ADJUSTMENT` match and bank transaction → `UNMATCHED` in the same transaction.
- `BankTransaction`: `UNMATCHED ↔ MATCHED`. `Reconciliation`: created `IN_PROGRESS`; `IN_PROGRESS → FINALIZED` stays EXISTING until S5.

## Alternate and error flows
- Bank credit 1,250.00 (09-12) against ledger 1,000.00 (09-11) and 250.00 (09-12) → `ONE_TO_MANY`, justification, `ACCEPTED`; bank 99.99 vs ledger 100.00 → `toleranceUsed = 0.01`, justification; bank 99.50 → `MATCH_AMOUNT_MISMATCH` (§4.6).
- Ledger line dated 10-02 against a window ending 09-30 → 409 `RECONCILIATION_LINE_INELIGIBLE` (M3; the bank row carries forward); ledger 09-02 against bank 09-25 outside `W` → 422 `MATCH_REQUIRES_REVIEW` with `DATE_OUT_OF_WINDOW`, then `ACCEPTED` with justification (§4.6).
- Line in an active match elsewhere, or a `PENDING` bank row → 409 `RECONCILIATION_LINE_INELIGIBLE` (D19); item on a matched line or `OUTSTANDING_CHECK` on a positive line → `OUTSTANDING_ITEM_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (§8.3).
- Adjustment for a bank date in a CLOSED period without override → `PERIOD_CLOSED` when the request date is closed, success when open; hard-locked with `overrideJustification` → `PERIOD_HARD_LOCKED` (§8.3).
- Same `requestId` twice → one JE, `replayed: true`; same `requestId`, different amount → `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. Reverse twice → `ADJUSTMENT_ALREADY_REVERSED`.
- `OTHER` without a link, with two links, or `settlesMatchId` on `BANK_FEE` → 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_REQUIRED`; blank or 9-character `OTHER` justification → 400 `JUSTIFICATION_REQUIRED` (§8.3).
- `settlesMatchId` on an exact match or with an amount other than the residual; a bridge on a statement without acknowledgement, with −45.00 instead of −45.67, or while `|openingDifference| ≤ 0.01` → 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; a second POSTED bridge → 409 `ADJUSTMENT_BRIDGE_ALREADY_POSTED` (§8.2, §8.3).
- `reaffirm` on a non-aged or non-`OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING` item; `clear-in-gap` called in a reconciliation whose statement has no acknowledgement, or on an item registered in this reconciliation → 422 `OUTSTANDING_ITEM_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; `clear-in-gap` without `approve` → 403 (§4.10, §8.3).
- `TRANSFER` validation failures in the order of behaviour 13; a residual or bridge with a sent `amount` that differs from the served value → 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (§3.5).
- A JE dated inside the window posted after creation → the next read shows a new unexplained ledger line and a moved `difference` (G4). Previous window `INVALIDATED` → `OPENING_DIFFERENCE` diagnostic, never "fixed" here (§3.7).
- A statementless create body (`windowStartDate`, `windowEndDate`, `closingBalance`, `openingBalance`, no `statementId`) → 422 `BANK_ACCOUNT_FEED_NOT_LINKED`: every phase-1 account lacks a feed link (§4.1, §4.10); the body is accepted only in phase 2.

## Acceptance criteria
1. **Given** no outstanding items **When** the review is read **Then** `glEndingBalance` equals `getAccountBalanceAsOf(statementEndDate 23:59:59.999999)`; a JE dated `statementEndDate` 23:30 is inside, one dated the next day 00:00 outside (E3, §8.1).
2. **Given** a deposit in transit +500 and an outstanding check −200 **When** the review is read **Then** `adjustedBankBalance = statementClosingBalance + 300` and `difference = 0` when the ledger holds both lines [M] — mutate the sign of the bank-side term (E3, §8.1).
3. **Given** a bank fee for a CLOSED month posted in the open month **When** both windows are read **Then** it is added exactly once to the closed window's `adjustedBookBalance` and not double-counted in the next [M]; **Given** two consecutive windows reconciled after both periods closed **Then** an August fee dated in October is in September's `sumLateAdjustments` and counted once in October (§8.1, C3, D7).
4. **Given** a predecessor that balanced through a late adjustment **Then** the successor's `openingDifference = 0.00` [M] (drop `sumOpeningAdjustments`); **Given** a deposit in transit registered in the predecessor and cleared in the successor **Then** it still counts at the successor's start [M] (test status `OPEN` instead of `closedOn`); a predecessor approved at 0.01 gives no `OPENING_DIFFERENCE` flag (§8.1, O2).
5. **Given** Dr cash 100 posted on day A and reversed with the reversal dated B > A **When** `getAccountBalanceAsOf` is read **Then** 100 from A to B − 1 and 0 from B [M] — restore the `POSTED`-only filter and it reads 0 before B and −100 from B; with the reversal dated A it reads 0 from A (G15, §3.7, §8.1). **Given** a reversed gap bridge and a reversed `ADJUSTMENT`-matched fee **Then** `countUnexplainedLedger` is unchanged [M] — count the reversal entry's line and it rises by one — while the fee's bank transaction returns to `UNMATCHED`, counts on the bank side and moves `difference` (§8.1).
6. **Given** an account whose first acknowledged statement starts 2026-09-01 and 500 POSTED cash lines dated before it **When** the first window is read **Then** `baselineDate = 2026-09-01` and `countUnexplainedLedger = 0` [M] — drop the lower bound and the count becomes 500 (§3.7, §8.2).
7. **Given** a 1:1 exact match **Then** `ACCEPTED` without justification; 1:N or N:1 **Then** `MATCH_REQUIRES_REVIEW` until justified; N:M **Then** `MATCH_CARDINALITY_NOT_ALLOWED` [M] (§8.3, M2, M5); the candidate fixture yields the documented scores and a tie proposes nothing; `auto-match` never yields `ACCEPTED` + `RULE` [M] (§4.6, M6, D12).
8. **Given** an `ACCEPTED` match unmatched with a reason **Then** the header stays with `state = UNMATCHED`, audit `RECONCILIATION_UNMATCH` carries actor and reason, and the ledger line can be matched again (M7, U4, §8.3).
9. **Given** the same adjustment `requestId` twice **Then** one JE, `replayed: true`, found by `findBySourceEvent(nameUUIDFromBytes(…))` [M] (§3.5, §8.3, G9); **Given** an adjustment with `bankTransactionId` **Then** an `ADJUSTMENT` match links its cash line and the bank row is `MATCHED`; **When** reversed **Then** both return to `UNMATCHED` (§4.7, §8.3).
10. **Given** an unlinked `OTHER` **Then** 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_REQUIRED` and no JE [M] — drop the check and the plug posts; **Given** a deposit in transit left unregistered **Then** `countUnexplainedLedger = 1` [M] (the refusal at approval is S5's) (§3.5, §8.3).
11. **Given** five 1:1 matches of 99.99 against 100.00 (`difference = −0.05`) **When** each residual is settled with an `OTHER` naming `settlesMatchId` and no `amount` **Then** each posts exactly the served residual −0.01 and is replaced by an exact three-member match (`replacesMatchId`, `toleranceUsed = 0`, old match `UNMATCHED` / `RESIDUAL_SETTLED`), `difference = 0.00`, and no line is unexplained at any commit point [M] — skip the replacement and the test fails; a `MANY_TO_ONE` match keeps its kind (§4.6, §8.3).
12. **Given** an acknowledged statement with `openingDifference = −45.67` **When** an `OTHER` with `bridgesStatementId` and no `amount` is posted **Then** it posts −45.67, Dr 2360 / Cr cash through the EXISTING mapping, `difference` and `openingDifference` become 0.00 and its cash line is not unexplained [M]; when the period of `statementStartDate − 1` is CLOSED it lands in the earliest OPEN period and enters `sumOpeningAdjustments` [M]; a second bridge → 409, also refused by the partial unique with the service check removed; a sent amount of −45.00 → 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_NOT_ELIGIBLE` (§4.2, §8.2).
13. **Given** `BANK_REC_OTHER_APPROVAL_THRESHOLD` unset **When** a caller holding only `adjust` posts an `OTHER` of 5.00 **Then** 403 `RECONCILIATION_ADJUSTMENT_APPROVAL_REQUIRED` [M] — treat unset as unlimited and the test fails; a residual of −0.01 → 201; with the key at 50.00, 50.00 → 201 and 50.01 → 403; with `approve` → 201 (§4.7, §8.3).
14. **Given** an `OTHER_LEDGER_TIMING` item older than the aging days **Then** it counts in `countUnexplainedLedger` until reaffirmed in this reconciliation and counts again in the next window [M] — compare against any past reaffirmation instead (§3.6, §8.3); **Given** `clear-in-gap` on an `OPEN` item registered before an acknowledged statement **Then** `CLEARED_IN_GAP`, `closedOn = startDate − 1`, and it leaves both terms (§3.6, §8.3).
15. **Given** `TRANSFER` **When** `counterGlAccountId` is absent, or sent on `BANK_FEE` **Then** 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_REQUIRED`; the reconciled account or an unknown one → 422 `ADJUSTMENT_LINK_NOT_ELIGIBLE`; an inactive one → 422 `GL_ACCOUNT_NOT_ACTIVE`; `1090 Undeposited Funds` → 422 `ACCOUNT_NOT_RECONCILABLE`; +250.00 posts Dr reconciled / Cr counter with no mapping lookup and −250.00 the inverse; zero → 422 `RECONCILIATION_ADJUSTMENT_SIGN_INVALID`; `GET /adjustment-types` serves `TRANSFER` [M] — drop the counter CHECK and a `TRANSFER` row without a counter persists (§3.5, §8.3, D9).
16. **Given** a row of 250.00 on 09-12 under duplicate review **Then** rows of 250.00 on 09-10 (same reference), 09-12 (same description) and 09-15 (neither) are its candidates in that order, while 250.01, a row dated 09-16 or an `EXCLUDED` row never is (§4.5, §8.4). **Given** a match of bank 99.99 against ledger 100.00 **Then** it serves `toleranceUsed = 0.01`, `residual = −0.01`; bank 100.00 against 99.99 serves `residual = +0.01` (§3.4, §8.3).
